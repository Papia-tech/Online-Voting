Essential Components for a Trusted Election

Comprehensive verifiable cryptography and secure infrastructure to run and monitor reliable, transparent, and private digital elections.

End-to-End Verifiability

Voters can independently check that their vote was recorded correctly and included in the final tally, all without revealing their choice.

Cryptographic proofs ensure full transparency from ballot casting to tabulation.

Maximized Voter Privacy

Leverage advanced privacy-preserving techniques like **Zero-Knowledge Proofs** and homomorphic encryption to protect every voter's choice.

Ballots are encrypted on-device and remain encrypted throughout the process.

Immutable Audit Trails

Every action and vote is recorded on a tamper-proof distributed ledger (blockchain), providing an unalterable record of the election.

Secure logging protects against data manipulation or retroactive changes.

Digital ID & Eligibility

Robust digital identity checks prevent duplicate voting and ensure only registered, eligible individuals can cast a ballot in the election.

Integrates with national ID databases for real-time eligibility confirmation.

Threat and DDoS Mitigation

State-of-the-art security systems are in place to actively detect and automatically block denial-of-service and malicious attacks.

Our infrastructure is designed for high availability and resilience under stress.

Multi-Language Support

Ensure accessibility and inclusion with full support for multiple languages, making the voting process clear for all citizens.

The entire ballot interface dynamically adapts to the voter's preferred language.
